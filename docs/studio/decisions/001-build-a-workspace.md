# ADR 001 — Build the place where the person works

Status: implemented in the accepted personal edition. Date: September 13, 2026.

The first workload produced a grant draft and a lecture outline. Form limits and budget arithmetic passed, but Jai rejected the grant as devoid of humanity and the lecture as the wrong artifact. He needed a tool for collecting his own material and developing his lecture.

We changed the deliverable to a source library and an editable canvas. Human capture and arrangement are the main interaction; model suggestions are optional. We retained the rejected runs so the change can be understood.

The alternatives were another prompt-only rewrite, a static rendered outline, or model training. None addressed the missing working surface. The evidence identified a workload/specification failure, not a demonstrated quantization defect.

Acceptance moved from “generated a valid object” to actual capture, editing, persistence, reopening and usefulness to Jai. He subsequently described the app as helpful and super aligned. That is acceptance of this experienced artifact, not validation of a future collaboration product.
